HallWala Policies

Transparent rules for a secure booking experience.

1. Platform Role & Responsibilities

HallWala acts as an online aggregator platform connecting customers with marriage hall vendors. We do not own or manage any physical halls. The quality, services, and offline arrangements are the sole responsibility of the respective Hall Vendor.

2. Booking & Payment Terms

  • A 10% Token Amount of the agreed total rent must be paid online to confirm and lock the booking date.
  • The remaining 90% payment must be cleared strictly 7 days prior to the booking date directly to the vendor.
  • For urgent bookings (made 2 to 4 days prior to the event), the remaining 90% must be paid at least 24 hours before the event date.
  • Failure to clear the 90% due amount within the given timeframe will result in automatic cancellation of the booking, and the token amount will be forfeited.
🚫 Anti-Circumvention & Privacy Rule: Customers and Vendors are strictly prohibited from sharing direct contact numbers, email addresses, or social media handles before a booking is confirmed. Any attempt to bypass the platform will result in an immediate and permanent ban for both the customer and the vendor from the HallWala platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as Name, Email Address, and Phone Number strictly for creating your account, generating legal booking agreements, and sending booking notifications.

2. Payment Security

All online payments are processed through a secure, RBI-compliant Payment Gateway (Razorpay). HallWala does not store your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or sensitive banking details on our servers.

3. Data Usage & Sharing

Your contact details are kept fully confidential and are only shared with the specific Hall Vendor once a Token Payment is successfully made, enabling them to coordinate your event. We do not sell user data to third-party marketing agencies.

1. 24-Hour Free Cancellation Window

If a customer decides to cancel their confirmed booking within 24 hours of making the token payment, a 100% Refund of the token amount will be processed automatically.

2. Standard Cancellation Policy

If the booking is cancelled by the customer after 24 hours of the token payment, the 10% Token Amount becomes strictly Non-Refundable, as the vendor blocks the calendar and loses potential business for that date.

If the booking is cancelled due to the failure of paying the 90% remaining balance (7 days or 24 hours prior rule), the booking is voided and no refund will be issued.

3. Vendor Initiated Cancellation

In the rare event that a Hall Vendor cancels a confirmed booking due to an unavoidable emergency, the customer will receive a full 100% refund of the token amount paid. Refund processing takes 5-7 business days to reflect in the original payment source.
