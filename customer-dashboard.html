HallWala — Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: October 2026 | Applicable to all Registered Users and Event Organizers

1. Nature of Platform & Intermediary Disclaimer

HallWala operates strictly as a technology platform and neutral digital facilitator connecting prospective customers ("Guests/Clients") with banquet/marriage hall owners ("Vendors/Hosts"). HallWala is not an agent, contractor, or co-organizer. The booking contract is directly between the Customer and the Venue Owner. HallWala assumes no involvement, supervision, or liability regarding the actual execution of the contract.

2. Venue Quality, Catering, and On-Site Disputes

HallWala expressly disclaims liability for any deficiency in services occurring at the premises, including but not limited to electricity failure, backup generator failure, illumination, sound quality, decoration, air conditioning performance, cleanliness, or food catering quality. Any verbal altercation, physical dispute, or dissatisfaction is strictly a private civil matter between the Customer and the Vendor. HallWala holds zero legal accountability.

3. "Make an Offer" & Booking Confirmation Protocol

  • Offer Submission: Submitting a price offer via the platform does NOT constitute a confirmed reservation or date block.
  • Vendor Acceptance: The acceptance of an offer by a Venue Owner indicates price agreement only and does NOT finalize the booking.
  • Final Confirmation: A booking is legally confirmed only upon successful payment of the mandatory 10% Token Amount and issuance of an official HallWala Token Number and digital Agreement PDF.

4. Payment Deadlines for Remaining 90% Balance

  • Standard Bookings (Booked 10+ days prior): The remaining 90% balance must be paid in full at least 7 calendar days before the scheduled event date.
  • Urgent Bookings (Booked 3 to 5 days prior): The remaining 90% balance must be paid within 24 to 48 hours of token payment, and in all circumstances at least 24 hours prior to check-in.
  • Default: Failure to clear the balance within these stipulated timeframes will lead to immediate cancellation of the booking and forfeiture of slot reservation.

5. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Cancellation within 24 Hours: If a booking is cancelled within 24 hours of Token Payment, a 100% full refund will be processed to the original payment source within 7 working days.
  • Cancellation after 24 Hours: Strictly Non-Refundable. Once 24 hours lapse, the hall calendar is blocked for other paying clients, causing direct business damages to the Vendor; hence no token refund will be approved under any circumstances.

6. Property Damage and Guest Conduct

The primary customer booking the venue bears 100% financial and civil liability for any property vandalism, infrastructure breakage, electrical damage, or theft caused by attendees during the event. The Vendor possesses the lawful right to demand immediate on-spot financial reimbursement for any caused damages.

7. Anti-Circumvention and Platform Integrity

Any attempt by a customer to discover a venue via HallWala and subsequently bypass the platform to finalize the deal off-grid is a breach of service integrity. Users detected in deliberate fee/platform bypass, or engaging in suspicious activities, will face permanent hardware/mobile blacklisting.

8. Zero Harassment and Respect Policy

HallWala enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against abusive language, threats, intimidation, or harassment directed at venue owners or HallWala support personnel. Infraction of this clause warrants instant termination of the user account and may attract legal notices under the Information Technology Act.

9. Force Majeure (Unforeseen Events)

HallWala shall not be held responsible for cancellations or disruptions arising from acts of God, extreme natural calamities, sudden statutory lockdowns, curfew orders, strikes, or regional administration bans beyond control.

10. Compliance with Local Law & Noise Regulations

Adherence to local municipal limits regarding sound/DJ decibel levels, timing restrictions (e.g., 10:00 PM loudspeaker cutoff), firecracker usage, and alcohol/liquor permits is the joint statutory obligation of the host and customer. HallWala takes no liability for municipal fines or police interventions.

11. Governing Law & Legal Jurisdiction

These Terms and Conditions shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any legal dispute or proceeding arising under this platform shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Bokaro Steel City / Jharkhand.

HallWala — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Compliant with IT Act 2000 & DPDP Act

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

HallWala ("Platform", "We", "Our") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our users ("Customers", "Guests", "You"). This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures regarding the collection, handling, storage, and safeguarding of your personal and transaction data when you use our web portal and mobile dashboard.

2. Categories of Information Collected

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, and residential city.
  • Event Specifications: Intended event dates, guest count estimates, nature of ceremony (e.g., Wedding, Reception, Birthday), and proposed budget offers.
  • Transaction Data: 10% token payment transaction identifiers, digital timestamps, and gateway payment references.
  • Disbursement / Refund Bank Details: Masked account numbers and IFSC identifiers entered by the user solely for token refund processing.
  • Device & Log Information: IP address, operating system, browser type, and access logs gathered automatically for platform security.

3. Purpose of Processing Your Personal Data

We collect and process your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To enable seamless hall discovery and facilitate negotiations between customers and banquet hall owners.
  • To generate official digital Token Receipts and binding Booking Agreement PDFs.
  • To transmit vital transaction notifications, verification codes (OTPs), and booking status updates via SMS/WhatsApp.
  • To investigate fraudulent actions, unauthorized attempts to bypass the platform, or violations of platform guidelines.

4. Structured Two-Phase Vendor Data Sharing

  • Phase 1 (Negotiation & Offer Stage): Your personal phone number and direct identity remain strictly masked and private. Vendors only see event requirements and your pricing offer.
  • Phase 2 (Post-Confirmation Stage): Your direct contact information is revealed to the venue host only after the mandatory 10% token amount is successfully paid and the official Agreement PDF is generated, enabling on-ground coordination.

5. Payment Security & Financial Data Isolation

All financial transactions are conducted directly through RBI-authorized payment aggregators utilizing end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption. HallWala does not collect, record, or store debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords on its servers.

6. Zero Spam & Strict No-Sale Guarantee

We strictly honor your digital privacy. HallWala never sells, trades, rents, or commercializes user contact databases to third-party telemarketers, insurance agents, or unsolicited advertising agencies under any circumstances.

7. Data Retention & User Erasure Rights

Transaction records and agreement PDFs are retained as legally required for financial audit, taxation, and legal dispute defense. Users maintain the right to review, update, or remove linked withdrawal methods or submit account closure and data deletion requests through our support channels.

8. Information Security & Safeguards

HallWala deploys industry-standard technical measures, encrypted data transit, firewalls, and restricted administrative privileges to shield your records against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

9. Grievance Redressal Mechanism

In compliance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, users with privacy questions or grievances may contact:

Grievance Officer: HallWala Legal & Compliance Cell

This Privacy Policy is construed under the laws of the Republic of India. Any disputes arising from data handling shall fall within the exclusive jurisdiction of the judicial courts in Bokaro, Jharkhand.
